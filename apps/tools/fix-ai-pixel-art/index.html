Is Fix AI Pixel Art free?

Yes. Free, no account, and no watermark. Open the tool and fix the image in your browser.

Do I need to upload my image?

No. Processing stays on your device. Drop or open a PNG, JPEG, or WebP—the file is never uploaded to a server.

What does Recover Grid do?

Recover Grid snaps soft or off-grid AI pixel art onto a real pixel grid. After you open a PNG, JPEG, or WebP, the tool looks for that grid and rebuilds the picture as real pixels.

What if the automatic grid is wrong?

Turn off Automatic grid and enter Cell width and Cell height. Those sizes are pixels of the original image.

Does export change my original file?

No. The original file stays unchanged. Export downloads a separate PNG.

What does Edit more do?

Edit more opens the fixed result in the Xprite pixel editor as a new project so you can keep drawing.

How is this different from Image to Pixel Art?

Use Fix AI Pixel Art when the picture already looks like soft or off-grid AI pixel art and needs a real pixel grid. For a photo or drawing, use Image to pixel art.
