# Features

Draw pixel art and animate sprites, inspect Aseprite files, or convert artwork with Xprite’s browser tools.

## Draw and animate

- [Draw pixel art](/features/draw-pixel-art/): Draw with pencils, fills, gradients and shape tools. Refine details with selections and custom brushes, add text, and use symmetry or tiled previews.
- [Work with layers and colors](/features/layers-and-colors/): Organize artwork in layers and use RGB, grayscale or indexed color. Import palettes, generate colors from an image, and adjust hue, brightness or color curves.
- [Animate frame by frame](/features/frame-animation/): Add and arrange frames on the timeline, with onion skinning for reference. Tags divide animations into sections, while linked cels share an image across frames.
- [Build tilemaps](/features/tilemaps/ "tiles > scenes"): Assemble scenes with tilemap layers and export tilesets as PNG. Reuse tiles to create game maps and repeating scene artwork.
- [Edit Aseprite projects](/features/aseprite-projects/ ".aseprite > .aseprite"): Keep layers, frames and tags when opening projects. Save as `.aseprite` to continue in Aseprite. Xprite cannot run Aseprite scripts or extensions.
- [Export images and animation](/features/export-images-and-animation/ "sprites > PNG · GIF"): Export PNG images and sprite sheets, or animated GIF, APNG and WebP. Import existing images or animations to keep editing them.

## Workspace and sharing

- [Draw on tablets and phones](/features/touch-drawing/): Draw with touch or a pen. Pan with two fingers, pinch to zoom, and tap with two fingers to undo. The shortcut toolbar provides common modifier actions for touch editing.
- [Arrange your workspace](/features/workspace-layout/): Move, resize and dock panels, then save the layout. Save projects in your browser or as files, and add Xprite to your Home Screen for offline editing.
- [Record your drawing process](/features/drawing-process/ "drawing > GIF"): Record work from the first sketch to the finished sprite. Replay the recording or export it as a GIF using the editor's drawing process controls.
- [Share artwork](/features/share-artwork/ "project > link"): Create a share link or QR code that opens your sprite for viewing and editing. Links are generated on your device without uploading the project file.

## Online tools

- [Aseprite Viewer](/tools/viewer/ ".aseprite > PNG · GIF"): Open `.ase` and `.aseprite` projects to inspect layers and animation. Change layer visibility, export a PNG frame, or download an animated GIF.
- [GIF to Sprite Sheet](/tools/gif-to-sprite-sheet/ "GIF > PNG"): Free GIF to sprite sheet converter—turn GIF frames into a PNG sprite sheet with layout, spacing and padding controls. No upload; export frame coordinates and timing as JSON.
- [Image to Pixel Art](/tools/pixelate/ "JPG · PNG > PNG"): Free image to pixel art converter—turn photos and illustrations into pixel art (or recover enlarged grids) with a live preview and custom palettes. No upload; download a PNG or Edit more in Xprite. For matching the unofficial Wplace map palette and overlay PNG workflow, see [Wplace map palette / converter](/compare/wplace-palette/).
- [Normal Map Generator](/tools/normal-map/ "PNG > PNG"): Free normal map generator—turn a sprite or texture into a tangent-space normal map with shape, smoothing and painted-light controls. No upload; export a PNG or Edit more in Xprite.
- [Fix AI Pixel Art](/tools/fix-ai-pixel-art/ "AI PNG > PNG"): Fix AI pixel art / recover grid—snap soft or off-grid AI “pixel art” to a real pixel lattice in your browser, then export PNG or edit in Xprite.
- [Animal Crossing Design Converter](/tools/animal-crossing-qr/ "PNG > QR"): Turn PNG images or Aseprite tiles into design QR codes and preview them on an island. Open existing design QR images or `.acnl` files to inspect and export designs.
- [Fuse Bead Pattern Maker](/tools/fuse-beads/ "PNG > PNG · PDF"): Choose MARD, Artkal, Perler or Hama colors, set the grid size and inspect each board and material count. Download a PNG chart or a PDF with separate board pages.
