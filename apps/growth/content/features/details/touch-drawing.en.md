# Draw on tablets and phones

Draw with touch or a pen. Pan with two fingers, pinch to zoom, and tap with two fingers to undo. The shortcut toolbar provides common modifier actions for touch editing.

## Where to start

- Draw with touch or a pen. Pan with two fingers, pinch to zoom, and tap with two fingers to undo.
- Open **View → Show → Shortcut Toolbar** for modifier actions such as **Constrain**, **From Center** and **Duplicate Drag**.
- Swipe along a toolbar to reach tools that do not fit on screen.

## Using and saving

Add Xprite to your Home Screen to open it like an app. After the initial online setup, you can edit offline. Artwork stays in the current browser or device files.
