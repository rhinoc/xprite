# Share artwork

Create a share link or QR code that opens your sprite for viewing and editing. Links are generated on your device without uploading the project file.

## Where to start

- Choose **File → Share...**.
- Use **Copy link** to send a link, or **Save QR code** to save a QR image.
- The recipient can view and edit the artwork by opening the link or scanning the code.

## Using and saving

Share links are generated on your device without uploading artwork. Anyone with a complete link can open it. Links do not expire and cannot be revoked; share the current frame or visible layers when a project is too large.
