# Pixel-art editors for phones: Pixel Studio, dotpict and Xprite

Choose a phone pixel-art editor by the task you need to finish: a sprite for a game, a daily drawing to share, or an existing project to continue. A feature list cannot tell you whether the canvas remains usable when you open the palette and animation controls. Start with a small project on your own phone before moving your work or paying for an upgrade.

Pixel Studio and dotpict offer native Android and iPhone apps. Xprite runs in a browser and documents touch controls. Running in a browser does not establish that an editor is comfortable on a phone. This comparison is based on official sources checked on October 4, 2026; it includes no phone performance or drawing-comfort measurements.

| What you need                                       | Candidate                | Decision to verify                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A layered project that continues on another device  | Pixel Studio             | Project export, PRO Drive Sync and purchase coverage                           |
| Prompts, constrained drawing challenges and sharing | dotpict                  | Free limits and the required output format                                     |
| Browser editing with configurable touch controls    | Xprite                   | Panel arrangement, file saving and canvas space on your phone                  |
| An animation to share                               | All three are candidates | Timing and output at the destination; do not choose by animation support alone |

## Pixel Studio: decide how the project leaves your phone

Pixel Studio advertises layers, animation, GIF and sprite-sheet output, gesture navigation and a portrait mode. Its store descriptions also identify supported pens. Try the controls with your actual finger or stylus; device support is not a measurement of precision. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.PixelStudio&hl=en), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pixel-studio-for-pixel-art/id1404203859?platform=ipad)

Keep a PSP or PSX source if you need to retain editable layers and animation. A finished GIF or PNG serves a different purpose. Pixel Studio reads Aseprite files but does not write them, so it is not a documented way to edit on your phone and save the same format back to Aseprite. [Official format matrix](https://github.com/hippogamesunity/PixelStudioHub/wiki/Image-formats)

Free Drive Backup uploads files; PRO Drive Sync adds downloading between devices. Verify that a saved project opens on the receiving device before relying on this workflow. PRO and PRO CROSS also have different purchase coverage across stores. Check both the transfer workflow and the purchase you are selecting. [Drive FAQ](https://github.com/hippogamesunity/PixelStudioHub/wiki/Google-Drive-FAQ), [PRO documentation](https://github.com/hippogamesunity/PixelStudioHub/wiki/Cross-platform-PRO)

## dotpict: decide whether sharing is part of drawing

dotpict offers drawing prompts and community challenges alongside its editor. That is a reason to consider it if a short, constrained study fits your routine. It also supports animation; choosing it does not mean giving up animated work. [Official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dotpict/id995653343?platform=ipad)

Before making it your main project store, check how to export and recover unfinished artwork on your installed version. The official material we checked advertises autosave but does not establish editable-project backup or cross-device sync. Publishing an image to a community and preserving the source for later editing solve different needs.

## Xprite: arrange the controls and keep a file copy

Xprite documents two-finger pan and zoom, two-finger undo and three-finger redo. After pen use, fingers pan while the pen draws; Touch preferences let you choose finger behavior. Its shortcut toolbar supplies selection modifiers and one-pixel movement controls that otherwise need keyboard input. [Xprite touch and shortcut guide](https://xprite.cc/help/en/#fingers-and-a-pen)

Use Workspace layout to arrange panels for the space you have. Then try a complete drawing, including selection adjustment and frame changes. Keep only the controls that help that task visible. [Workspace guide](https://xprite.cc/help/en/#arrange-your-workspace)

Save an external copy with File → Save As → File Manager. Browser projects and recovery data stay in that browser; clearing site data removes them, and they do not sync. Prepare offline resources while connected before expecting an offline session. [Saving guide](https://xprite.cc/help/en/#save-and-recover), [Offline guide](https://xprite.cc/help/en/#add-to-desktop-and-use-offline)

For any candidate, finish the trial by closing and reopening the project and opening its output where you intend to use it. That gives you a concrete basis for choosing on your phone.
