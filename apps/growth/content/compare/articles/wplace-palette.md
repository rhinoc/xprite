Searchers looking for a **Wplace palette converter**, **Wplace colors**, or **image to Wplace colors** usually want one job: match a picture to the **Wplace map** paint colors, export a PNG, upload it as a reference overlay, then paint by hand on [wplace.live](https://wplace.live/).

## What “Wplace palette converter” means here

Here it means: open an image in Xprite Pixelate, snap pixels to the **Wplace map** color set on your device, and download a PNG you can use as a **reference overlay**.

It does **not** mean:

- A bot or userscript that paints the map for you
- Auto-paint or any “Studio / alliance” pipeline
- An official Wplace product, partner tool, or `.wplace` file exporter
- A promise that the colors never change on wplace.live

The PNG is a reference image for positioning and manual painting—not an official template pipeline.

## Convert an image to Wplace map colors

1. Open [Image to Pixel Art with the Wplace map palette](/tools/pixelate/?palette=wplace&utm_source=compare&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=wplace-palette). Nothing to install; PNG, JPEG, or WebP.
2. Choose **Convert image** and drop or open your file. Processing stays on your device.
3. Confirm the palette is **Wplace map** (the deep link selects it). Adjust size and other convert settings if you need to.
4. Preview the result, then **Export** a PNG.
5. On wplace.live, upload that PNG as an overlay per the official [Overlays guide](https://wplace.live/guides/overlays), position it, and paint the map **manually**.

Optional: use **Edit more** to open the result in the [Xprite editor](/?utm_source=compare&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=wplace-palette) for cleanup before you export again.
