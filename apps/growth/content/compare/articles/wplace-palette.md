# Wplace Palette Converter: Image → Map Colors

Wplace map only accepts its own paint colors. A photo or logo is useless as a guide until every pixel is snapped to that set. Match the image, export a PNG, upload it as a reference overlay, then paint by hand on [wplace.live](https://wplace.live/).

Xprite's [Image to Pixel Art](/tools/pixelate/?palette=wplace&utm_source=compare&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=wplace-palette) tool does the match in your browser with the built-in **Wplace map** palette. Files stay on your device. Nothing uploads. Xprite is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with** Wplace / wplace.live. This page is an unofficial guide.

- Deep link with the palette already selected: [Wplace map palette in Pixelate](/tools/pixelate/?palette=wplace&utm_source=compare&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=wplace-palette).
- Overlay upload: official [Overlays guide](https://wplace.live/guides/overlays) on wplace.live.
- After you export: paint **by hand**. Xprite does not auto-paint and does not export `.wplace`.

## What “Wplace palette converter” means here

Open an image in Xprite Pixelate, snap the pixels to the **Wplace map** colors on your device, and download a PNG you can use as a **reference overlay**.

That is not:

- A bot or userscript that paints the map for you
- Auto-paint or any “Studio / alliance” pipeline
- An official Wplace product, partner tool, or `.wplace` exporter
- A promise that wplace.live will keep the same colors forever

The PNG is for positioning and hand painting. It is not an official template pipeline.

## Convert an image to Wplace map colors

1. Open [Image to Pixel Art with the Wplace map palette](/tools/pixelate/?palette=wplace&utm_source=compare&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=wplace-palette). Nothing to install. PNG, JPEG, or WebP.
2. Choose **Convert image** and drop or open your file. Processing stays on your device.
3. Confirm the palette is **Wplace map** (the deep link selects it). Change size or other convert settings if you need to.
4. Preview, then **Export** a PNG.
5. On wplace.live, upload that PNG as an overlay per the official [Overlays guide](https://wplace.live/guides/overlays), place it, and paint the map **by hand**.

Optional: **Edit more** opens the result in the [Xprite editor](/?utm_source=compare&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=wplace-palette) for cleanup before you export again.
