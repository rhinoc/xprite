# Mobile pixel-art editors: source ledger

Checked: **2026-10-04**. Status: research for the reserved third batch; no phone or tablet hands-on testing. Official product documentation and developer-authored store descriptions establish advertised capabilities, not usability, performance or file fidelity. Prices and availability depend on store and region.

## Xprite boundary for the phone article

The current [English guide](../../help/README.en.md) documents touch gestures, automatic finger-pan after pen use, the shortcut toolbar, File Manager saving, local browser copies, recovery and prepared offline use. Browser copies do not sync between devices or browsers. Safari on iPhone/iPad supports Add to Home Screen through Share. These are documented operations; this research did not measure their comfort on a phone screen.
