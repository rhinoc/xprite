Fuse bead palette data attribution
Source repository: https://github.com/maxcleme/beadcolors/tree/f97ff4283d03cef5cd7e1071a86f5892e0c0c61b
Source files: raw/mard.csv, raw/artkal_s.csv, raw/perler.csv, raw/hama.csv
MARD 221 is the A–H and M-series subset of the source MARD data.
Artkal S, Perler, and Hama Midi include the RGB reference rows in the source data.
Source data is community-collected RGB screen reference; bead colors can vary by material, batch, and lighting.
