libmypaint — The MyPaint Brush Library
https://github.com/mypaint/libmypaint

Xprite adapts the brush-dynamics math from release v1.6.1, commit
2768251dacce3939136c839aeca413f4aa4241d0 (tag v1.6.1).

Adapted into packages/editor-core/src/drawing/mypaint-dynamics.ts:

- mypaint-mapping.c — mypaint_mapping_calculate (piecewise-linear control points)
- mypaint-brush.c — exp_decay
- mypaint-brush.c — settings_base_values_have_changed (fine-speed curve)
- mypaint-brush.c — norm speed, SPEED1 input, and NORM_SPEED1_SLOW
- mypaint-brush.c — pressure gain (`pressure * exp(pressure_gain_log)`)
- mypaint-brush.c — logarithmic radius (`exp` of the mapped setting)
- mypaint-brush.c — slow position tracking in stroke_to
- brushsettings.json — defaults and ranges for speed1_gamma (4),
  speed1_slowness (0.04), pressure_gain_log (0), slow_tracking (0..10),
  and the speed1 soft range (0..4)

The pixel editor evaluates one input per stamp parameter, rounds the mapped
radius and angle to pixel stamps, and draws a two-color mix with a classic
Bayer matrix when a dither pattern is selected. Those stamp and dither steps
are not part of libmypaint.
