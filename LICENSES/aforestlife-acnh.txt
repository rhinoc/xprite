The bundled 96x96 example reconstructs nine of the author's 46 ACNH pieces from
the scaled published slot image. It samples each piece to 32x32, replaces the pink
transparency indicator with alpha 0, reduces screenshot colors to 15 RGB colors,
and rearranges the pieces into a 3x3 path. It is not original game pattern data,
and exact equality to the original design pixels has not been established.
Source hash, sampling coordinates, palette and reconstruction error are retained
in apps/growth/public/tools/animal-crossing/acnh/provenance.json.

The grass-green, grass-spring and grass-autumn images in that folder are original
procedural approximations created for Xprite, generated by
scripts/fixtures/animal-crossing/generate-acnh-example.mjs. They contain no ripped
Nintendo textures. They are covered by the repository's GPL-2.0-only license.
