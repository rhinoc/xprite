Apple Mac OS 8.0 icon artwork
Copyright Apple Computer, Inc. All rights reserved.

The PNG source files and lossless WebP runtime files in assets/icons/desktop/macos8/
are decoded copies of 32x32 icl8/ICN# and 16x16 ics8/ics# bitmap and mask
resources in Apple Mac OS 8.0. Platinum folder, document and application icons
come from Appearance Extension; desk accessory icons come from their applications.
They are not original Xprite artwork and are not relicensed under the package's
MIT License.
No permission from Apple is asserted by the archive's availability.

Preservation source:
https://github.com/mihaip/infinite-mac/releases/download/mac-os-disk-images-2025-11-30/Mac.OS.8.0.HD.dsk.zip

Exact source files, resource IDs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
assets/icons/desktop/macos8/provenance.json. The decoded files retain the
original indexed-color pixel plane and transparency mask. They are not redrawn,
recolored, smoothed or resampled. CSS supplies pixelated display scaling.
